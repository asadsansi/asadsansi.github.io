What the app does

The app scans QR codes and barcodes with your phone’s camera or from an image, shows what they contain, keeps a history and favourites on your phone, makes your own QR codes, and, if you tap “Look up product”, fetches details about a product barcode. You can back up your history to your own Google Drive if you switch that on.

What the app is not

The app shows what a code contains; it does not check whether that content is safe or correct. A QR code can lead to a website, a payment page or a Wi-Fi network chosen by whoever made the code. Look at the address the app shows before you open it, and do not enter passwords or payment details on a page you do not trust. Product details come from a public database and may be missing or wrong; check the product’s own label for anything that matters, such as allergens.
