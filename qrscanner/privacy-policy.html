Privacy policy

CheckScan (Android, package com.siliconbytes.checkscan) · Effective 28 October 2026

In short: CheckScan has no ads and no advertising ID. Your scans stay on your phone unless you turn on backup. The app sends data out only when you start it yourself — a product lookup you tap, or a backup you switch on — plus anonymous usage and crash diagnostics that never include what you scan.

Who we are

Asad Ullah Sansi. Contact: asadullahsansi@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your phone

CheckScan keeps your scan history, any inventory counts you create, your favourites and your settings in local storage on your device. There is no account and no sign-in. A scanned Wi-Fi password is shown to you but is never saved to history or included in any backup.

What we collect

CheckScan uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, and Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive diagnostics when the app crashes. This data is anonymous app-usage and stability information. It does not include the content you scan, generate or type, and the app does not use an advertising ID: advertising-ID collection is turned off and the advertising-ID permissions are removed from the app.

Product lookup

When you tap "Look up product" on a scanned barcode, and only then, CheckScan sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public database of grocery products, to fetch product details. No other information is sent, and nothing is sent until you tap.

Backup to your Google Drive

Backup is off until you turn it on in Settings. When you enable it, CheckScan asks your permission and then copies your scan history and inventory counts to a private application folder in your own Google Drive (the Drive "app data" area). Only CheckScan on your account can read that folder; the developer cannot. You can turn the backup off, or delete the data, at any time. Wi-Fi passwords are never included.

Permissions

Camera — to scan codes; you can also scan a saved image without granting it. Internet — used only for the product lookup and the Google Drive backup described above. CheckScan requests no storage, location, contacts or microphone permission.

Purchases

If you buy CheckScan Pro, the purchase is handled by Google Play; the developer never receives your payment-card details.

How your data is protected

Data on your device is held in the app's private storage. Network requests (product lookup, Drive backup) use encrypted HTTPS connections. Because there is no account, there is no profile of you held on any server by the developer.

Backup to your Google account

Android's own backup may copy your history, counts and settings to your Google account so a new phone starts where you left off. Wi-Fi passwords are excluded. You can turn this off in Android Settings under System, Backup.

Exporting and sharing

You can export your scan history to a CSV file and share QR codes you create. These are actions you take; the file or image goes only where you send it.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

History and counts stay on your device until you delete them, which you can do per item or all at once in the app. Uninstalling removes all local data. If you used Drive backup, delete it from Settings or remove CheckScan's app data in your Google Drive. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for the periods described in Firebase's documentation.

Children

CheckScan is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal profile and asks for no personal details.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, this page is updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
